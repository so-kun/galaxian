# Fidelity checklist

What is reproduced exactly, what is modelled, and what is authored. Kept honest
so nobody has to reverse-engineer this project to find out.

## Modelled — right structure, approximate response

- **The discrete audio.** The three sections, the divider taps, the shared LFSR
  and the separate HIT/FIRE paths are all structurally correct, but the 555
  timers and op-amp filters are modelled with simple oscillators and one-pole
  filters rather than simulated. See the caveat below.
- **`CALCULATE_TANGENT`.** The original approximates the ratio by repeated
  shifting; we compute it directly and quantise to the same 24 headings. Same
  headings, possibly different rounding at the boundaries.
- **Subpixel resolve.** Stars are box-filtered 3:1 on the way to the canvas,
  which is the physically honest reading of a beam that is on for a third or
  two thirds of a dot, but is not a CRT model.

## Authored — no ROM available

- **All tile and sprite artwork.** The GFX ROMs cannot legally be distributed.
  What is *not* invented is the layout: every ordinal and sprite code matches
  the disassembly, so the game logic addresses graphics exactly as the original
  does. Rotation frames are generated by supersampled rotation of a base shape
  rather than hand-drawn per angle.
- **The 32 colour PROM bytes.** Chosen to land on the hardware's quantisation
  lattice (8 red × 8 green × 4 blue) and to match the colours the game is known
  to display. `Palette.loadColorProm()` replaces them if a real dump appears.
- **Attack pacing and enemy fire rate.** The slot allocation and difficulty
  arithmetic are the original's, but how often a sortie is triggered and how
  often a diving alien shoots are tuned by feel, not extracted.
- **Attract mode.** Not implemented.

## Known gaps

- Sound is not, and with current public knowledge cannot be, identical to the
  real board. MAME's own TODO still says the background hum is too slow and the
  explosion too hard, and its audio device notes that CD4066 switch mixing
  (input impedance changing to >10 MΩ) cannot be reproduced by a discrete core.
  Getting closer needs recordings from real hardware and the sound schematic.
- The arc table's exact 25-step quarter is stored rather than derived. It is
  characterised as a radius-16 quarter circle and the test checks that geometry,
  but the original rasterisation algorithm was not recovered — a greedy
  error-minimising walk reproduces only 16 of its 25 steps.
- Horizontal blanking start is unresolved upstream (`galaxian.h` carries a
  commented-out `HBSTART = 264 * XSCALE`); we follow MAME's 256-dot crop.
- Whether the star LFSR is reset by nVSYNC or free-runs is disputed; we follow
  MAME's free-run.
